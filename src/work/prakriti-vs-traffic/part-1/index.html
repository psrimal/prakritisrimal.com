Prakriti vs Traffic

Jayanagar to Whitefield is 20 km on a map and a lot longer in my morning.

Prakriti Srimal   ·   25th October, 2026.

I've lived in Bengaluru all my life and, until recently, I had the luxury of living within 15 minutes of school, college and work.

Now I live in Jayanagar and my office is in Whitefield.

20 km in Bangalore traffic. Torture.

The metro has made things easier. At least I'm not stuck on a road waiting for people to move.

Before I even open my eyes

Before my eyes are fully open, I'm already asking the same questions.

  • Auto today? Will traffic build up?
  • Which metro route gets me there fastest?
  • Can I make the transfer without sprinting across platforms like my life depends on it?

If you've seen a girl running at full speed across platforms and escalators, constantly checking her watch, that's me. If I waste a minute I might miss the train. Or worse, the next one will be so crowded that my only source of oxygen is whatever passes through someone's hair.

That's a lot to get through before my morning coffee, and worse after a tough training session.

So I decided to stop complaining and solve it.

One week, six trips

I started with the simplest data I could collect. Each trip, I screenshotted what Google Maps promised before I left and, when I remembered, ran a stopwatch for what actually happened. That's 14 screenshots from six trips. These are the two trips where I got all three screens.

Stopwatch times are for the trips I remembered to time. The 48:11 is an auto. The 1:29:24 is the metro, with an auto for the last stretch home instead of a walk that would have added about 20 minutes.

Two things jumped out. The metro barely moved: 1 hr 29 to 1 hr 34 on all six trips. The drive never sat still. The same morning trip was quoted anywhere from 37 minutes to a full hour, depending on when I left. A slow but predictable option against a faster but unpredictable one is exactly what I want to untangle.

What I'm building

Google gives me one number per mode. I want to know which choice is best today. So for one week, as an experiment, I'm building a small tool on the Google Maps API and BMRCL's GTFS data. It does four things:

  • Metro Finds the best metro-only route to office and estimates how crowded the train will be.
  • Auto Estimates the auto time for the same trip.
  • Segments Splits the road into segments. A direct auto estimate is always shorter than what really happens, so I treat each segment as its own origin and add them up.
  • Compare Puts them all side by side so I can decide before the coffee.

I'll write down the experiments and the rabbit holes as I go, and see whether a week is enough to find a smarter way across Bengaluru.

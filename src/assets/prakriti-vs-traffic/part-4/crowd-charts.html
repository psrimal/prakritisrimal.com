The quickest journey is not always the one with a seat. For every day and departure time of week 3, this page scores how crowded the pick is, and what a few extra minutes would buy.

How crowded is the quickest journey?

At each day and departure time, the pick is the entry and exit pair with the shortest door-to-door time. Its crowding is the seat index of the train on every stretch of the ride, weighted by ride time: 100 is an empty train, 50 is every seat taken, 0 is seats and standing room both full. The loads are typical weekday ridership, so they vary by time of day, not by the individual day.

What a few minutes buy

For every departure time, I also looked for a calmer journey that gets me to the office no more than minutes later than the quickest. Each table shows how often such a journey exists, how much time it costs, and how much more comfortable it is. Comfort is the seat score from 0 to 100, where higher is calmer (50 means every seat is taken). The day-by-day rows show the average journey time and the seat score of the quickest journey, then of the calmer one.
